Questions people ask

Short answers first.

Is the tea caffeine free?

Kadha Calm, Hibiscus Dusk and Turmeric Gold are caffeine free. Tulsi Dawn contains a small amount of green tea.

How long does delivery take?

Bengaluru orders arrive in 1 to 2 days. The rest of India takes 3 to 6 days. Shipping is free above ₹499.

How do I brew tulsi tea?

Use 1 teaspoon per cup. Steep in water just off the boil for 4 minutes.

Can I return a tin?

Yes. Unopened tins can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a refund.
